How I track this website

A short guide to the method. This is also a long page, so I can test scroll depth tracking.

Watch: an introduction

Start with business questions

Do not start with tags. Start with the questions that the business must answer. For example: which channels bring people who contact us? Each question becomes one or more events.

Take your time with this step. Write down what you see in GTM Preview mode and in GA4 DebugView. Compare it with your tracking plan. If they are not the same, fix the tag before you continue.

Write the tracking plan

Use a sheet with these columns: business question, event name, parameters, trigger, GTM tag and status. Use the GA4 recommended event names when one fits. This makes reports easier to use.

Take your time with this step. Write down what you see in GTM Preview mode and in GA4 DebugView. Compare it with your tracking plan. If they are not the same, fix the tag before you continue.

Install Google Tag Manager

Put the first GTM code in the head of each page, as high as possible. Put the second code just after the opening body tag. Then add a Google tag with your GA4 Measurement ID.

Take your time with this step. Write down what you see in GTM Preview mode and in GA4 DebugView. Compare it with your tracking plan. If they are not the same, fix the tag before you continue.

Use the dataLayer

The dataLayer is a list that the page uses to send information to GTM. This site sends the page type on each page. The contact form sends an event called generate_lead.

Take your time with this step. Write down what you see in GTM Preview mode and in GA4 DebugView. Compare it with your tracking plan. If they are not the same, fix the tag before you continue.

Build one tag at a time

Make the tag, test it in Preview mode, check it in DebugView, then publish. If you build many tags at the same time, errors are hard to find.

Take your time with this step. Write down what you see in GTM Preview mode and in GA4 DebugView. Compare it with your tracking plan. If they are not the same, fix the tag before you continue.

Test and document

After each change, write a version name and a short note in GTM. Keep a log of what you changed and why. This helps you and the next analyst.

Take your time with this step. Write down what you see in GTM Preview mode and in GA4 DebugView. Compare it with your tracking plan. If they are not the same, fix the tag before you continue.
